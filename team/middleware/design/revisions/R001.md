# R001: Revision 1, MVP scope (2026-10-06)

**Decision round 1:** the PO answered "all recommended" (Q1–Q10 A, see TODO.md). Research: `research/mvp-scope/README.md`.

What changed (Revision 0 → 1), with the reason for each:
- **The fleet-api adapter moves into this scope** (Q1 A). tesla-api-connection round 2's recommended answers apply: code in `src/`, client + mappers, a `TESLA_FLEET_BASE_URL` env, and `src/lib/tesla.ts` replaced (it rotated the refresh token on every call).
- **History comes from `calendar_history`, fetched hourly; one `live_status` per hour goes to `samples`; `/home` makes a live call cached 60 s** (Q2 A, Q5 A). That's about 1,440 req/month, inside Tesla's $10 credit.
- **A separate worker process** (Q3 A, CLAUDE.md D5). It is bundled with esbuild and runs as a second compose service.
- **Energy and weather rows are never pruned** (Q4 A, the PO's many-years Energy history requirement). ADR 0001 stands.
- **Forecast = baseline:** load from the median of the last 7 days; solar from Open-Meteo GHI × the site's learned ratio (Q6 A, CLAUDE.md §7 minimal).
- **Location and temperature unit come from env** (Q7 A): Tesla gives no coordinates.
- **The tariff stays in Tesla's `tariff_content` shape**, parsed by one pure module (Q8 A). This answers the front-end's open question. The day-of-week convention (0 = Monday) is inferred from the SCE TOU-D-4-9PM structure.
- **Replay = a seed script over the fixtures** (Q9 A).
- **Middleware owns the new migration** (Q10 A).
- **Lead's simplifications:**
  - no `job_runs` table: health comes from data freshness;
  - weather is fetched every 3 h;
  - `weatherTomorrow` is derived from hourly forecast rows (no daily table);
  - the SoC projection has no inverter power cap;
  - `endsAt` is found by a 15-min scan.
