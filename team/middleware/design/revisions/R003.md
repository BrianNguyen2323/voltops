What changed (Revision 2 → 3):
- **Energy history module** (`src/server/history`). Before: 4 modules queried `energy_intervals`/`weather_observed`, and "complete day" was defined twice (worker `≥ slots`, peak SQL `= slots`; equal under the PK). Now one Site-bound handle owns both tables, with Site-local dates in its interface. Pure restructure.
- **Site sync.** Before: only the login callback read `site_info`, by hand-written fetches with a hard-coded NA base URL, so tariff/reserve/capacity went stale until a re-login. Now `listSites`/`getSiteInfo` are on the DataSource, one `syncSite` writes the Site row + tariff, and login + a daily worker job call it (~30 req/month).

Lead's choices (inside the PO's answers):
- **`listSites()` joins `getSiteInfo()`** on the DataSource: login has to find the energy site id, and CLAUDE.md §5 already lists it. Same products call as today; no new cost.
