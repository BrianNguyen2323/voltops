# R002 — Revision 2: M1 review changes (2026-10-06)

## Changed
- **Right now:** the flow diagram becomes an **orthogonal cross**:
  - Solar top, Grid left, Home right, Powerwall bottom, hub in the centre;
  - only horizontal and vertical lines, with equal arms that touch their nodes;
  - each node is one unit (icon/ring, then name, kW and state beneath);
  - the Powerwall node is its SoC ring.
- **Tonight:** adds a "Tomorrow" weather tile, inset inside the card. The data contract gets `weatherTomorrow` (°C canonical) and `site.temperatureUnit`. The formatter gets `formatTemp`.
- **48 h chart:**
  - plain language: no "p10/p90"; the band is called "Expected range for home use", with a one-line caption;
  - home use is a solid line (dashed after Now), the range is a hatched band, solar is an amber area;
  - a custom HTML legend with matching swatches, plus a "kWh" y-axis title.
- **Cost today:** a negative net reads "Credit earned $0.79", never "-$0.79".

## Why
- M1 review by the PO (TODO.md "Review: M1", answer b, items 1–4).
- The B003 reviewer nits: the ring was far from its label, the lines stopped short, no y-axis unit, and the money minus sign.

## Lead decisions inside the PO's direction (the PO can override at the M2 review)
- **Cross layout:** it follows the subject's own convention (Tesla's app draws the same four nodes around a centre). Equal orthogonal arms fix the "different angles".
- **Weather contract:**
  - temperatures are stored in °C, like Open-Meteo's default and like W/Wh are canonical;
  - `site.temperatureUnit` only picks the display unit; the sample uses "F" (US site);
  - expected solar is **not** in the weather object. It's derived from the timeline's day-2 forecast, so the tile and the chart can't disagree.
- **Band styling:** a texture difference (hatched area vs solid line) rather than a new colour. The home colour keeps its meaning (SPEC design rule 4: one colour per energy type).
- **Brief size:** one brief (B005) covers contract + UI, to save a tester/implementer/reviewer cycle. The changes are small and touch the same few files.
