# R003 — Revision 3: M2 review changes (2026-10-06)

## Changed
- **Right now, mobile:** the kW readings in all four flow nodes go one type step smaller below 640 px. All four use the same class. Each node gets ≥ 6 units of inner padding (this also fixes the B005 nit about the ring being flush with the top and the state words being tight to the bottom).
- **Right now, price strip:** the bare "Peak" badge is replaced by two labelled items.
  - "Grid price now": $0.48/kWh, peak rate until 9:00 PM.
  - "Your home now": spending, saving and earning per hour, each with a one-line plain explanation. A pure `costNow` helper does the maths.
- **Chart tooltip:** it uses the legend's swatch component, so the expected-range marker is the same hatch in both places.

## Why
- The PO's M2 review (TODO.md "Review: M2", answer b, items 1–3):
  - the badge didn't say what it was;
  - "$0.48/kWh until 9 PM" read like it could be a total;
  - the PO wants the home's current cost in money.

## Lead decisions inside the PO's direction (the PO can override at the M3 review)
- **"Saving"** = the home use not drawn from the grid, valued at the current import price. That is what the home would pay right now without solar and battery. It matches the PO's example ("the 2.1 kW your home is currently saving or spending"). In the sample it is 2066 W × 48¢ ≈ $0.99/hour.
- **"Earning"** is shown separately, from export at the export rate ($0.13/hour in the sample), so the saving and earning figures never double-count.
- Rates are shown **per hour** because the inputs are instantaneous power.
- The **Tomorrow-tile indent** nit wasn't picked by the PO. It stays open.
