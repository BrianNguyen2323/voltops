# R008 — Revision 8: Your system + Equipment health (2026-10-08)

## Changed
- **Snapshot** gains two required fields:
  - `system`: batteries, solar panels, plan, firmware, install date, change log. null = not synced.
  - `equipmentHealth`: solar clear-day output vs a year ago, a sunny-day miss, estimated battery capacity, round-trip efficiency, backfill progress.
- **View** derives the status per check (OK / Watch / Check / Learning) from fixed thresholds owned by the front-end. It also derives system size and best hour per panel.
- **UI:** a "Your system" section at the bottom of `/home`: Batteries, Solar and Plan tiles with health chips, an ⓘ with the math, and a details line plus change log.
- **Sample values** for all of it. A `build.ts` stub (`system: null`, health learning) until the middleware lands B036–B038.
- **Middleware backlog:**
  - B036: site_info fields, panel env vars, best hour, the change log;
  - B037: the pure health functions;
  - B038: a one-time backfill inside a monthly request budget.

## Why
- PO topic 4 (chat, 2026-10-08): show the system's batteries, panels, panel rating and storage. Then the PO added the goal of monitoring health ("a decline in generation from the panels or the storage of the batteries").
- Decision round 4: Q1–Q4 A ("the outline looks good").
- Decision round 5: Q5, Q7, Q8 and Q9 A. Q6 A, on the PO's condition: a **one-time** backfill of all existing data, within Tesla's request limits, leaving enough for normal use.
- That gives the budget rule:
  - backfill ≤ 2,500/month;
  - the monthly total stays ≤ 4,500 of the ~5,000 free (500 Data requests per $1, $10 credit);
  - 1 request per minute at most.
- Tesla has no panel data, so panel count and rating come from `SITE_PANEL_COUNT` / `SITE_PANEL_WATTS`.
- Research: `research/system-info/README.md`, `HEALTH.md`.

- No Settings form for panels yet (env vars until Settings exists).
- The optimization round is still due; it runs after M8.
