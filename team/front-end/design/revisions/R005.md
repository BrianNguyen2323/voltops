# R005 — Revision 5: deepen the DashboardData seam (architecture candidate 1) (2026-10-06)

## Changed
- The `DashboardData` contract (Rev 1–4) splits in two:
  - a **Dashboard snapshot** (inputs only, from an adapter);
  - a **Dashboard view** (derived).
- Both live in one pure module, `src/lib/dashboard.ts`, with `checkSnapshot` (returns a list of problems) and `toDashboardView`.
- **Snapshot changes:**
  - drops `today` and `last7Days`;
  - adds `previousDays` (6) and a per-hour grid/battery breakdown on actual hours;
  - TSDoc everywhere;
  - `live` is the instantaneous reading at `updatedAt`.
- **Day rule:** today/tomorrow come from each hour's site-local date. The Timeline must cover the whole of both days (47–49 rows).
- **View** adds `today`, `last7Days` and `solarOutlook`, so the UI stops slicing the Timeline by index.
- **Page:** logs `checkSnapshot` problems and still renders.
- **Hand-off:** `docs/dashboard-snapshot.md` (+ a copy in `team/middleware/`).
- **New `GLOSSARY.md`:** Site, Home use, Live reading, Site-local day, Timeline, Dashboard snapshot, Dashboard view, Energy history.

## Why
- The architecture review (`research/architecture/README.md`, candidate 1, Strong) found:
  - hidden index rules ("row 24 is tomorrow");
  - stored-but-derivable totals;
  - invariants tested only against the fake.

  The middleware team is about to become the second adapter at this seam.
- The PO picked candidate 1 and answered Decision rounds 2–3: Q1–Q12 all as recommended (TODO.md), then confirmed the design summary.

## Not changed / deferred
- `costToday` stays an input until the tariff module (architecture backlog item 3).
- Time formatting stays where it is (backlog item 2, candidate 3).
- **New PO requirement, Energy history** (years of per-Site weather + kWh for degradation checks): handed to `team/middleware/`. It conflicts with the CLAUDE.md §6 90-day raw retention combined with ADR 0001, which that team must resolve.
