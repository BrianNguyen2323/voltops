## Lead clarification of Q2 (applying the decision; no new choice)
Q2 says to keep every hex and rename to shadcn's names. In shadcn, `accent` is a **subtle hover background** for menus and ghost buttons. If it were the old pink #ff0066, every hover would turn hot pink. So:
- shadcn `--accent` = #202026 (subtle);
- the pink #ff0066 lives on as `--destructive` and `--chart-4` / `--color-grid-import`. That matches its meaning on the dashboard: money leaving the house, and alerts.

Nothing used the `accent` class before this change (grep, 2026-10-06). The PO can override this at the M1 review.
